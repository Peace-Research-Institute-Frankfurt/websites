Prof. Dr. Malte Göttsche is professor for Peace Research in Natural Sciences at PRIF and TU Darmstadt, co-speaker of CNTR, and head of the research group Science for Nuclear Diplomacy. He researches nuclear non-proliferation, arms control and disarmament with a focus on the development of verification methods and technologies.
