<Leadin>The convergence of AI </Leadin>and the life sciences has continued to accelerate, while the policy debate around these tools has become increasingly concrete. Over the past year, the discussion has shifted from broad concern about AI-enabled biological design toward questions of who should be able to access these tools, under what conditions, and subject to what forms of oversight.
