<Leadin>Since the publication </Leadin>of the previous CNTR Monitor, several developments have reinforced and, in some areas, intensified these structural dynamics. The past year has been characterized by three overarching trends: the geopoliticization of critical raw materials, new bottlenecks in AI hardware supply chains, and the continued fragmentation of global technology governance. At the same time, the rapid expansion of AI infrastructure has created new strategic vulnerabilities related to energy supply, data center capacity, and the geographic concentration of advanced semiconductor manufacturing.
