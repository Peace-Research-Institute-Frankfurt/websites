Dr. Yan-Jie Schnellbach heads the junior research group “nuSENTRY” (Neutrino-based Safeguards for Emerging Nuclear Technology and Reactor Types) at TU Darmstadt. His research focuses on nuclear non-proliferation, with an emphasis on detection and measurement technologies.
